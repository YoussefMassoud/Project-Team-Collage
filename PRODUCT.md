# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Social media managers who oversee brand accounts across platforms (Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok). They need fast, reliable analysis of post performance without switching between multiple tools. Their context is: juggling multiple posts, short attention windows, data-driven decisions under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

Reachify ingests a social media post URL and returns AI-powered sentiment analysis, engagement metrics, issue detection, improvement suggestions, and an auto-generated video report. It eliminates the manual cross-referencing between native analytics, sentiment tools, and reporting. Success is a manager who can paste a link and get an actionable brief in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Confident, sharp, data-driven. The tone is direct and precise. No hype, no fluff. The interface earns trust through clarity and consistency, not decoration.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards (HubSpot, Salesforce). No heavy card grids, no decorative illustrations, no gradient-heavy hero sections. Reachify should not look like a template. Also avoid: social media platform clones (Instagram, TikTok), over-gamified neon aesthetics, corporate sterile greys.

## Design Principles

1. **Data is the hero.** Every visual decision serves the clarity and readability of insights. If an element doesn't make data easier to understand, it doesn't belong.
2. **Trust through precision.** Social media managers bet on this analysis. Precise typography, consistent interaction patterns, and confident layout build that trust. Ambiguous UI erodes it.
3. **Disappear into the task.** The interface is a lens, not a destination. Users come for the analysis, not the chrome. Strip everything that doesn't serve the workflow.
4. **Earned confidence, not flash.** No decorative AI tropes. No gradient text, no glassmorphism as default, no orchestrated load sequences. Substance over spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, and screen reader support across all interactive components. Reduced motion respected.
